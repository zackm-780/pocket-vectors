FIRST FLIGHT
Start on a runway. Hold B to test the brakes, then double-tap B for the parking brake.
Push the right stick up to add power. The throttle stays where you leave it.
Steer with the right stick on the ground, and the left stick in the air.
Rotate when the airspeed is alive, then climb away and raise the flaps with D-pad left.
A toggles the gear. Y cycles the HUD. Start opens the pause menu.
Select+Start quits. The Menu key also acts as Select.
Hold L2 and use the right stick or the D-pad to look around. L2 + L3 switches to the chase view.

FLIGHT LOG on the main menu lists every flight: when, the aircraft, where from and to,
how long and how it ended. A shows the details. The log is conf/flightlog.csv.
