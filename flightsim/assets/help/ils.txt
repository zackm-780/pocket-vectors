ILS APPROACH
Load a plan that names an approach, or pick one from the approach list.
On the map or the plan screen, the localizer id is shown when the runway has an ILS.
Hold L1 and press Y so NAV/APR is in approach. The HUD shows LOC and GS when the signal is alive.
Fly the needles, or let the autopilot capture. The glideslope is the vertical needle.
Start types of 2, 6 and 10 NM final place you on a 3 degree path already.
