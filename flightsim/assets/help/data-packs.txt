DATA PACKS
Terrain and the navigation database live in flightsim/data/.
A pack needs data/nav/nav.ini and, for terrain, data/terrain/pack.ini.
Releases attach two packs: pdx_oth (Portland to North Bend) and sea_sfo (Seattle to San Francisco, which holds all of pdx_oth). REGION.txt in the pack names its box and main airports.
Build one with tools/datapack/build_datapack.sh. Presets include pdx_oth, sea_sfo, bayarea, mthood and tam_diablo, or pass a bounding box.
Copy the pack into flightsim/data/ and restart. ATTRIBUTION.txt from the pack is shown on the About page.
Without a pack the game still flies the synthetic airport XTST.
