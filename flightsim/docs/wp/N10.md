# N10: Fly-by turn arcs between GPS legs

Phase 2c · Effort: high · Depends: the 1.3 fixes (#15, #16, #17) · Branch: `wp/N10-flyby-arcs`

## Why

The owner (2026-11-08): "The GPS tracks don't do arcs, so the turns to intercept a waypoint are very
sharp and not realistic. Ideally flight plans have realistic arcs between waypoints."

Today the navigator sequences a fly-by waypoint early (`shouldSequence`, anticipation
`R tan(dPsi/2)`), but the next leg's guidance is the straight TF line from the waypoint, so the AP and
the FD intercept it from inside the corner: the cross-track jumps by about R at every sequence, the
FD cue jumps to full bank, and a jet on short legs overshoots by up to 1 NM (`FdFollowTest`: the
737 on the 4.8 NM XTST legs). The map, the route overlay and the mini nav draw sharp corners.

This card makes the turn an arc tangent to both legs, flown with a bank feed-forward and drawn
wherever the route is drawn. Do not bump the version: the owner decides between 1.2.4 and 1.3.

## Do

1. **Geometry** (`src/nav/FlyBy.h`, pure, no allocation). Work in a local flat frame around the fix
   (east/north metres; turns are a few NM). 
   - `double flyByRadiusNm(double gsKt, double bankDeg)`: `R = V^2 / (g tan(bank))`, V from
     `max(gsKt, 40)` (the navigator's `kAnticipationMinGsKt`).
   - `struct FlyByTurn { bool valid; LatLon fix, start, end, center; double radiusNm, turnDeg
     (signed, + = right), inCourseTrueDeg, outCourseTrueDeg, anticipationNm; }` and
     `FlyByTurn flyByTurn(const LatLon& from, const LatLon& fix, const LatLon& to, double radiusNm)`:
     in-course = bearing from `from` to `fix` at the fix, out-course = bearing `fix` to `to`,
     `turnDeg = wrap180(out - in)`; `anticipation = R tan(|turn| / 2)`; start (T1) on the inbound
     leg and end (T2) on the outbound leg, each `anticipation` from the fix; centre `R` from T1 on
     the turn side. Not valid when `|turn| < 1` or `|turn| > kMaxFlyByDeg = 135`.
   - The arc must fit: when `anticipation` exceeds half of the shorter leg, shrink R to
     `0.5 * shorterLeg / tan(|turn|/2)` (the bank then rises; the AP clamps it).
   - `struct ArcGuide { double xtkNm; double dtkTrueDeg; double progress01; bool done; }` and
     `ArcGuide arcGuide(const FlyByTurn&, const LatLon& pos)`: `xtk` relative to the arc with the
     NavState sign (+ = right of the path: for a right turn, inside the circle), `dtk` the tangent at
     the aircraft's bearing from the centre, `progress01` the swept angle over `|turn|`, `done` when
     it reaches 1.
   - `int arcPoints(const FlyByTurn&, LatLon* out, int maxPoints)`: points every 10 degrees,
     both ends included, for drawing.
2. **Turn bank.** `Simulation` passes the AP's `bank_limit_deg` to `Navigator::setBankLimitDeg`
   after `setAircraftCategory` (today only the category's 20 / 25 is used). The fly-by bank is
   `min(25, bankLimit - 5)`: 15 degrees for the 20-degree pistons, 20 for the jets, so the AP keeps
   5 degrees for wind. `shouldSequence` uses the same radius (one helper), and adds half the
   roll-in, `gs * 2.5 s`, to the anticipation so the roll starts before the arc does.
3. **Navigator.** When `shouldSequence` sequences a fly-by fix (not `flyOver`, the outbound leg a TF
   and the inbound a TF, DF or IF, `|turn| <= 135`; the same rules it already uses), build
   `turn_ = flyByTurn(start of the inbound leg, fix, end of the outbound leg, radius)`. While
   `turn_` is valid and the active leg is the outbound leg, `update` takes the lateral guidance
   from `arcGuide` (DTK, XTK, the CDI from that XTK) until `done`, then drops `turn_` (the TF line
   takes over with XTK near 0). Drop it as well when `|xtk| > 1 NM` (way off the arc: the NAV
   intercept takes over), and in `setPlan`, `clearPlan`, `loadApproach`, `directToLeg`,
   `activateLeg`. Turns over 135 degrees keep today's behaviour (sequence at the fix, intercept).
4. **NavState** (additive): `bool turnActive`, `float turnBankDeg` (signed feed-forward bank for
   the arc at the present ground speed, + = right, `atan(V^2 / (g R))`), `float turnRadiusNm` (the
   radius the navigator would use at the present ground speed for the next turn, published every
   update, for the drawings), `float legTrackTrueDeg` (the active leg's own course; equal to
   `desiredTrackTrueDeg` outside a turn).
5. **NAV law** (`lateralBankCmd`, `LatMode::Nav`): when `nav.turnActive`, the command is
   `turnBankDeg` plus the existing look-ahead correction on the arc's XTK and DTK, clamped to the
   bank limit as now. The FD shows the same command, so a pilot following it flies the arc. LOC and
   HDG are unchanged.
6. **Drawing.** One helper in `FlyBy.h` gives, for leg `i` of a plan, where its line starts and
   ends (T2 of the turn at fix `i-1`, T1 of the turn at fix `i`, or the fixes when there is no turn)
   and the turn into it; all three drawers use it with `nav.turnRadiusNm` (the overlay gets it as a
   new `buildWorldOverlay` / `WorldOverlay::render` argument, 0 = sharp corners as now):
   - `MapPage::drawRoute` and `MiniNav::draw`: the leg line from start to end, the turn into the leg
     as a polyline in the leg's style (magenta for the active leg, dashed where the leg is dashed).
   - The overlay route ribbon: `addSegment` per arc piece, heights interpolated along the leg.
   Turns over 135 degrees keep the sharp corner. No allocation per frame: fixed point arrays.
7. **Nav box.** During a turn the nav box shows the DTK of the leg being turned onto
   (`legTrackTrueDeg`), so it does not count through every degree; the heading-tape DTK bar keeps
   `desiredTrackTrueDeg` and slides round with the turn.
8. **Docs.** NAVIGATION.md §3: the fly-by turn (radius, bank, limit, when it ends); autopilot
   README: the NAV row gets the feed-forward; ARCHITECTURE §5: one line per NavState field.
