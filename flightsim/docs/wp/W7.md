# W7: Cloud layers (outline)

Phase 3 · Effort: high · Depends: X3c (GPU headroom), X5 (live METAR) · Who: a planner session
refines, then a builder

## Goal

Up to three cloud layers from the METAR sky condition (FEW, SCT, BKN, OVC with base heights),
drawn as flat textured layers that fade with distance. Inside a layer, the fog closes in. Above
an overcast, the ground disappears. That makes ILS approaches to minimums meaningful.

## Constraints to plan around

- The device budget: fill with blending and the fog shader is about 311 Mpix/s, roughly 17
  full-screen layers per 16.7 ms (DEVICE.md §2). One cloud layer seen from below covers most of
  the screen. Plan for at most two blended full-screen passes, and one draw call per layer.
- No float textures on the device. Generate the cloud texture once at start, as RGBA8.
- The Low preset may draw clouds as fog only.
- Weather already parses the METAR (`src/world/WeatherMetar.*`). Check whether it keeps the sky
  condition before planning new fields.

## Planner instructions

Read `src/world/Sky.h`, `src/world/Atmosphere.h`, `src/world/Weather.h`, the X3c results and
DEVICE.md §2. Then rewrite this card in the board format, with a GPU-cost acceptance item
measured with `--bench`.
