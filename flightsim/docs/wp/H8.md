# H8: Map page terrain shading and holds (outline)

Phase 3 · Effort: medium · Depends: X3c (map frame cost on the device) · Who: a planner session
refines, then a builder

## Goal

Two H4 follow-ups that the status line in WORK_PACKAGES lists:
1. **Terrain shading** on the map page: a coarse elevation tint (green → brown → white, water
   blue) under the route, so the Coast Range and Mt Hood read at a glance.
2. **Hold racetracks**: draw holding patterns from procedure hold legs (HM, HA, HF) at their fix,
   with the inbound course and turn direction.

## Constraints to plan around

- The map is drawn with `Renderer2D` (HUD batch). Terrain shading should be a small texture
  (for example 128×128) built from `TerrainQuery` off the main thread when the map pans or zooms
  by more than a quarter screen, and uploaded once. Never sample terrain per frame.
- The map's frame cost on the device comes from T2 and X3c. Shading must stay under 1 ms of GPU
  time and add one draw call.
