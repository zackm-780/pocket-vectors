# U1: Quick flight and landing score

Phase 2 · Effort: medium · Depends: OD3 · Branch: `wp/U1-quick-flight`

## Why

Every feature added after 1.0 (overlays, mini nav) starts off, so a first-time player sees none
of it. Nothing gives a reason to fly the same approach twice. This card is a suggestion, not in
PLAN.md; it waits for the owner's yes.

## Read

- `src/ui/SetupScreens.cpp` lines 100–130 (main menu rows) and how NEW FLIGHT starts a flight
  (`grep -n 'START FLIGHT\|startFlight\|MenuAction::Start' src/ui/*.cpp`)
- `src/app/Scenario.h` (fields for aircraft, airport, runway, start, distance, plan, approach)
- `src/audio/AudioControl.cpp` around line 93 (how touchdown is detected today)
- `src/app/App.cpp`: `grep -n 'messages.post\|AppState::' src/app/App.cpp | head -n 30`
- `assets/help/first-flight.txt`

## Owns

New `src/app/LandingRating.{h,cpp}`, new `tests/app/landingRatingTest.cpp`, new
`src/ui/QuickStart*` (if the menu code needs a new screen), `assets/help/first-flight.txt`.

**Also touches:** `SetupScreens.cpp` (a QUICK FLIGHT row), `App.cpp` (call the rating at
touchdown and post the message), `src/core/Paths.*` only if a helper for `conf/logbook.csv` is
missing.

## Do

1. QUICK FLIGHT on the main menu: the first row when there is no last flight, otherwise right
   after RESUME. It starts the c172p on a 6 NM final to KPDX 28L with the ILS armed, and the
   route overlay and mini nav on **for that flight only** (settings.ini is not changed). Without a
   data pack, it falls back to XTST.
2. Landing score at the first touchdown: vertical speed (fpm), distance past the threshold or
   aim point, centerline offset, bank and crab. Grade A–F with a short reason
   (`B  -240 FPM  12 FT LEFT`), shown for 8 s.
3. Append a row to `conf/logbook.csv`: UTC time, aircraft, airport, runway, values, grade. The
   write happens after touchdown, never per frame.
4. Mention QUICK FLIGHT on the Help page "first flight".
