# F10: Armed speed brakes (auto deploy on touchdown)

Phase 1.4 · Effort: medium · Depends: none (after H9 and F9 on the board: shared hot files) ·
Branch: `wp/F10-speedbrake-arm`

## Why

The owner (2026-11-09): arm the speed brakes on the aircraft that have them (`speedbrake = true`:
737, 787-8, A320, B747, MD11, global5000). Armed, they deploy fully when the main gear touches down
with the thrust at idle, and stow when the thrust goes up (a go-around). Today X cycles retracted
→ 50 % → 100 % → retracted (`PilotControls.cpp`), and the flight model already raises the ground
spoilers when the speedbrake is over 50 % on the ground (`FlightModel.cpp`, `cSpoiler`).

## Do

1. **Cycle** (X, keyboard `/`) on aircraft with speedbrakes: RETRACTED → ARMED → 50 % → FULL →
   RETRACTED. Messages `SPEEDBRAKE ARMED`, `SPEEDBRAKE 50%`, `SPEEDBRAKE FULL`,
   `SPEEDBRAKE RETRACTED`. ARMED keeps `speedbrakeNorm = 0`. Any manual position clears the arm.
   No new action: the owner asked for ARM in the existing `SpeedbrakeCycle`.
2. **Auto logic** (`SpeedbrakeArm`, a small struct with `update(const AircraftState&, const
   AircraftProfile&, ControlInputs&)` that returns a message or null; no allocation):
   - deploy (`speedbrakeNorm = 1`, still armed, message `SPEEDBRAKE DEPLOYED`) when armed, the
     aircraft has been airborne since it was armed (latched, so arming on the ground at idle does
     not deploy them on the spot), `wowAny`, and every thrust lever ≤ 0.05; with the thrust above
     idle at touchdown they deploy the moment it comes back to idle;
   - stow (`speedbrakeNorm = 0`, disarm, message `SPEEDBRAKE STOWED`) when deployed by the arm
     and any thrust lever goes above 0.5 (go-around, on the ground or after a bounce);
   - X while auto-deployed retracts and disarms, as today;
   - the latch resets on a new flight (the `Simulation` start).
3. **Where it runs:** in `Simulation::stepOnce` on `pilot_`, after `script_.apply` and before the
   autopilot, so A/T thrust levers (copied back by `syncPilot`) count, and headless runs see it.
   `Simulation` has no message queue: keep the last message in a small fixed buffer
   (`const char* lastSpeedbrakeEvent()` plus a counter) and let `App.cpp` post it to its
   `MessageQueue` after `sim.advance` (one line in **Also touches**).
4. **Scripts:** `speedbrake_arm = <t>, 1` (arm at time t) in scenario `[script]`, for the tests.
5. **HUD:** the speedbrake status field shows `SPDBRK ARM` (green, the normal role) when armed and
   retracted, and today's `SPDBRK <n>%` when out (white, amber with thrust over 50 %). The text must
   fit the field's slot (10 cells, `kSlots[0]`); widen it only if the HUD layout test still passes.
6. **Headless scenarios:** a 737 final from 2.5 NM with the A/T off, speedbrake armed, the fleet
   pilot's flare and idle at 20 ft (reuse `FleetPilot.h` from a test, or a `Simulation` scenario
   with `[script]` throttle and elevator), and a go-around: armed, touch down, full thrust 2 s
   later.
