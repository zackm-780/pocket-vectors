# N9: SIDs and STARs (outline)

Phase 3 · Effort: high · Depends: N8 · Who: a planner session turns this into one or more
builder cards

## Goal

Pick a departure (SID) and an arrival (STAR) for US airports from FAA CIFP, the same way
approaches are picked today, and fly them with LNAV and the altitude constraints shown.

## What exists

- The pipeline (`tools/nav/cifp.py`) and the runtime format (`procedures/{ICAO}.proc`, DATA §7)
  carry approaches only (`APPROACH` blocks).
- `.fms` files may name a SID and a STAR. They are kept in the header (`planToFms` copies them)
  but never flown.

## Likely split

1. N9a, pipeline: emit `SID` and `STAR` blocks with transitions (DATA §7.2, additive: format
   version 1 readers skip unknown blocks, so check `Procedures.cpp` before relying on that).
2. N9b, runtime: `Procedures` exposes SIDs and STARs, and the navigator builds their legs like
   approach legs (TF, CF, DF, VA and CA at minimum; holds as in H8).
3. N9c, UI: the flight plan screen gets DEPARTURE and ARRIVAL rows. `.fms` import loads the named
   SID and STAR.

## Planner instructions

Read DATA §7, `src/nav/Procedures.h`, `src/nav/ApproachBuilder.h` and `tools/nav/cifp.py`. Then
write N9a to N9c as cards in the board format and add them to the table in README.md.
