# S2: Audio, no crackle, voice callouts

Phase 2 · Effort: medium · Depends: none (part B waits for owner decision OD1) · Branch:
`wp/S2-audio-callouts`

## Why

- The device logged an ALSA underrun with the 512-sample buffer (DEVICE.md §2, audio row;
  `src/audio/AudioEngine.cpp` sets `want.samples = 512`).
- Terrain warnings and altitude callouts are tones only (`App.cpp`, `gpwsToneBuf`). Spoken "SINK
  RATE", "PULL UP", "MINIMUMS" and "FIVE HUNDRED … TEN" are much of what makes an approach feel
  real.

## Read

- `src/audio/AudioTypes.h`, `src/audio/AudioEngine.cpp` (88 lines), and in `AudioSynth.cpp` the
  mixer entry point (`grep -n 'void .*mix\|render' src/audio/AudioSynth.cpp`)
- `src/hud/GpwsMonitor.h` lines 25–70 (`GpwsEvent`: `kind`, `valueFt`, `text`)
- `src/app/App.cpp`: `grep -n 'gpwsToneBuf' -B4 -A22 src/app/App.cpp | head -n 60`
- `src/app/Settings.h` (the audio fields) and the `VOLUME` row in `src/ui/FlightScreens.cpp`

## Owns

`src/audio/`, `tests/audio/`, new `assets/audio/callouts/README.md` (file names, format, licence
rule).

**Also touches:** `App.cpp` (replace the inline tone mapping with a call into `audio/`),
`Settings.*` and `assets/settings_default.ini` (`voice_callouts`, `audio_buffer_samples`),
`FlightScreens.cpp` (a VOICE CALLOUTS row).

## Do

**Part A (ready now)**
1. `audio_buffer_samples` (default 1024 when `FLIGHTSIM_PLATFORM=portmaster`, otherwise 512).
   Log the buffer size that was obtained.
2. A clip player: `AudioEventType::Clip` with a clip id. Clips are 16-bit mono PCM WAV at the
   engine rate, loaded once at start from `assets/audio/callouts/<name>.wav` by a small RIFF
   reader (no dependency). All clips together stay under 1 MB of RAM. A missing or bad file falls
   back to today's tone and logs it once.
3. Move the GPWS-to-sound mapping into `audio/` as a pure function
   (`calloutFor(const GpwsEvent&)` → a clip id plus the fallback tone). Clip names:
   `sink_rate`, `pull_up`, `terrain`, `too_low_gear`, `too_low_flaps`, `minimums`, `2500`,
   `1000`, `500`, `100`, `50`, `40`, `30`, `20`, `10`.
4. `voice_callouts = true|false` (default true; with no clip files you hear tones, as today).

**Part B (after OD1)**
5. Add the clips the owner supplies or approves, with their licence in `port/licenses/` and a
   line in the About credits.
