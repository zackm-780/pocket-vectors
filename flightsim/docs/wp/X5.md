# X5: Live METAR at launch

Phase 2 · Effort: medium · Depends: P1b, OD4 · Branch: `wp/X5-live-metar`

## Why

Weather reads `data/weather/<ICAO>.txt` (`world::loadMetarFor`, called from
`src/app/Simulation.cpp`). Today those files come only from `tools/weather/fetch_metar.py` run on
a PC. Its own docstring says the launch script could call it where `python3` exists, but
`port/FlightSim.sh` never does. The handheld has Wi-Fi.

## Read

- The P1b lines in a device launch log (`python3`, SSL version, `curl`, `wget`, DNS)
- `tools/weather/fetch_metar.py` (stdlib only; `--out`, `--plan`)
- `port/FlightSim.sh` from the `GAMEDIR` setup to the binary launch
  (`grep -n 'GAMEDIR=\|exec\|flightsim.aarch64' port/FlightSim.sh`)
- `port/package_port.sh` (what gets copied into the port)
- `src/world/WeatherMetar.h`, and the weather rows in `src/ui/FlightScreens.cpp`
  (`grep -n 'METAR\|WEATHER' src/ui/*.cpp`)

## Owns

`tools/weather/`, the fetch block in `port/FlightSim.sh`, `port/package_port.sh` (ship
`fetch_metar.py`), `port/test_launch_scripts.sh`, `src/world/WeatherMetar*`, `tests/world/`
(METAR age), `flightsim/INSTALL.md` (a "Live weather" note).

**Also touches:** `Settings.*`, `assets/settings_default.ini` (`weather_live_fetch`, default from
OD4), `FlightScreens.cpp` (a LIVE WEATHER row).

## Do

1. In `FlightSim.sh`, when `weather_live_fetch = true` in `conf/settings.ini` and `python3` with
   SSL exists (`curl` or `wget` as a fallback), start the fetch **in the background** with
   `timeout 15` before launching the game. Never delay or fail the launch. Log one line with the
   result.
2. Stations: departure and destination from `conf/last_flight.ini`, the ADEP and ADES of each
   `data/plans/*.fms`, plus an editable `conf/metar_stations.txt` (created with KPDX, KOTH, KEUG,
   KSLE, KCVO, KTTD, KHIO, KAST). Add `--last-flight` and `--stations-file` options to
   `fetch_metar.py`.
3. In the game: compute the METAR age from its day/time group and show it where the weather is
   chosen (`METAR KPDX 25 MIN`). Older than 3 h: still applied, shown as `OLD`.
4. If P1b shows the stock OS has neither `python3` with SSL nor `curl`/`wget`, stop. Document
   the PC route in INSTALL.md and report "not possible on the stock OS".
