# F8: PA28 balked landing with full flaps

Phase 1.4 · Effort: medium · Depends: F5 (shares `assets/aircraft/*.ini` and `tests/fleet/`) ·
Branch: `wp/F8-pa28-balked-landing`

## Why

The owner (2026-11-09): with full flaps the PA28 keeps descending at full power, above the stall
speed. `assets/aircraft/pa28.ini` (`[speeds_kt]` comment) already notes the cause: in the stock
JSBSim `pa28` model the flaps pitch the nose down beyond the elevator's authority (idle, flaps
40: the 1 g "stall" is 68 kt at only 3.7° alpha, more than the 61 kt clean).

A real PA-28-181 climbs, weakly, in that configuration: it is certified to a balked-landing climb
with takeoff power and the flaps in the landing position (CAR 3 / FAR 23.77: a 3.3 % gradient,
about 200 fpm at 65 kt). The POH's balked landing is full power, flaps to 25°, then 10° once
climbing. The planner found two suspect lines in the stock model:

- `aero/coefficient/CmDf` (pitching moment due to flap): 0 / −0.0654 / −0.0981 / −0.1140 at 0 / 15 /
  25 / 35° flap, against `Cmde` −0.603 per radian of elevator and `Cmalpha` −1.023 per radian.
- `aero/coefficient/CLDf` (lift due to flap): 0 / 0.065 / **0.016** / 0.254 at 0 / 15 / 25 / 40°.
  The 25° value is out of order (0.16 was likely meant).

## Do

1. **Measure first.** A fleet phase `sim: fleet <id> balked` that starts trimmed on a `final`
   start (or `cruise` at 3,000 ft with `ias_kt` set) with the given flap detent, sets full
   throttle (mixture as the profile starts), holds wings level and holds each target IAS of
   65, 70 and 75 kt with the elevator (a slow IAS-hold loop on pitch, 30 s settle, then the mean
   VS over 20 s). It prints `FLEET pa28 balked_vs_fpm_f<detent>_<kt> <value>` plus the elevator
   position, alpha and pitch it needed. Run it for the PA28 at flaps 40, 25, 10 and UP, and for
   the c172p at flaps 30 (the c172p's full flap) and 10 as a reference.
2. **Decide.** If the PA28 at flaps 40 cannot hold a positive rate at any of the three speeds, or
   the elevator sits at its stop, the model is wrong (the certification rule above). Then fix it
   in `assets/jsbsim-overlay/aircraft/pa28/pa28.xml`: a full copy of the stock file with the
   header line `<!-- OVERLAY (flightsim WP F2): copy of the stock JSBSim v1.3.3
   aircraft/pa28/pa28.xml. Changes: … -->` (keep that exact prefix: the overlay test looks for
   it). Change as little as possible: first `CmDf` (reduce the nose-down moment until the
   elevator holds 1 g at 1.1 × the flaps-40 stall with a margin of at least 20 % of its travel),
   then the `CLDf` 25° entry. Note every change and the reason in the header comment. Never
   touch the stock files under `build/_deps`.
3. **Targets with the fix** (full power, sea level ISA, the model's shipped weight): flaps 40
   between +100 and +400 fpm at the best of 65–75 kt; flaps 25 above flaps 40; flaps 10 above
   flaps 25; the clean climb within 10 % of today's (the fix must not change the clean airplane).
4. **Re-measure the stall** with the fix (`stallTest` for the pa28). With the elevator no longer
   the limit, set `stallElevatorLimited` to false if the full-flap stall now comes below the
   clean one, and update `vs0`, `vref` / `vapp` only if the fleet rules (Vapp ≥ 1.15 × the
   approach stall) now ask for it. Update the `[speeds_kt]` comment in `pa28.ini` and the pa28
   row in `tests/fleet/README.md`.
5. **If a real PA-28 could not hold level** either (only if step 1 shows the model is within the
   certification rule), change nothing in the model and add the line to
   `assets/help/first-flight.txt` instead: `Go-around in the PA28: full power, flaps 25 at once,
   then flaps 10 once climbing. Full flaps will not climb.` Add that line in either case if the
   flaps-40 climb stays under +200 fpm, with the last sentence as `Full flaps climb poorly.`
6. Keep every existing fleet test green (all ten aircraft; the pa28 landing, approach and
   autopilot flights use flaps 40).
