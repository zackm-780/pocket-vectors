# W8: Taxiways from OpenStreetMap (outline: W8a, W8b, W8c)

Phase 4 · Effort: high · Depends: W1b (#15) merged, OD6 (answered 2026-11-08: OpenStreetMap)

## Why

The owner (2026-11-08): "I'd like to have taxiways... having taxiways makes realism great so you
don't have to just back taxi on runways." The packs have runways only; airports show a generic
apron and a short taxiway stub (`buildAirportExtras` in `src/world/AirportGeometry.cpp`).

Read [../TAXIWAYS.md](../TAXIWAYS.md) first: sources and coverage (KPDX well mapped, 46 of 145
taxiways named, no widths; KOTH 19 ways, no names), the ODbL terms, the pack format, the
rendering and the flattening plan. PLAN.md D23 records the source choice.

## Outline (a planner turns each part into a ready card with Read, Owns, Do, Acceptance)

### W8a: extraction, pack format, loader

- `tools/nav/osm_taxiways.py` (standard library): Overpass per airport box, cached; filters and
  width defaults from TAXIWAYS.md §3; writes `data/nav/taxiways.csv`, `aprons.csv`, and
  `TAXIWAYS-ODbL.txt`; the ODbL line in `ATTRIBUTION.txt`. Hooked into `build_datapack.sh` and
  the `datapack` workflow (an `--osm-taxiways` switch, on by default).
- DATA.md §5: the two files; §2: the OSM row (added with the plan as "planned").
- `CsvNavDatabase`: optional load into additive `Airport::taxiways` and `Airport::aprons`
  (`core/NavTypes.h`, one line each in ARCHITECTURE §5). Older packs load unchanged.
- Tests: the tool on a saved Overpass response fixture (< 100 KB, under `tools/nav/tests/`); the
  loader on a synthetic CSV fixture; a pack without the files still loads.
- Hot files: `tools/nav/`, `src/nav/NavDb*`, `core/NavTypes.h`.

### W8b: smooth ground under taxiways

- `world/terrain/FlattenSet`: a uniform grid index (about 500 m cells) so a lookup tests only
  the areas near the point; keep the W1b composition and its tests.
- Taxiway segments as graded flatten areas with `priority = -1`; aprons flat; heights from the
  runway surface where a segment meets a runway, else the smoothed DEM clamped to the field's
  runway elevations ± 5 m (TAXIWAYS.md §3).
- Tests: the W1b crossing tests extended to a taxiway meeting a runway (no step, ≤ 1:100 on both
  pavements); a lookup-time test (KPDX-sized synthetic set: 3 runways, 600 segments, under 2 µs
  a lookup on the desktop).
- Hot files: `src/world/terrain/`, `src/world/AirportGeometry.*`.

### W8c: drawing

- Taxiway ribbons with fillets at shared nodes, aprons as polygons, on the composed surface; the
  yellow centerline and edge lines; one or two draws per airport; not drawn beyond 5 NM.
- The generic apron and taxiway stub only at airports without OSM taxiways.
- The About page: "Taxiways © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)".
- Acceptance: screenshots at KPDX (the 28L threshold, the terminal ramp from above in the chase
  view) and KOTH; triangle and draw counts in the PR; no new allocation per frame.
- Hot files: `src/world/AirportGeometry.*`, `src/world/AirportRenderer.*`, `src/ui/HelpScreens*`
  (About).
