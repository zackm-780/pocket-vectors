# H9: V-speed and flap bugs on the speed tape

Phase 1.4 (was 3) · Effort: high · Depends: X3c, F5 · Branch: `wp/H9-speed-bugs`

## Why

The profiles carry the speeds (Vs0, Vs1, Vr, Vref, Vapp, Vfe per detent, the takeoff and approach
detents), but the speed tape shows only the colour bands and the autopilot's speed bug. The owner
wants (2026-11-07):

- V1 / VR / V2 on the takeoff roll and in the climb; Vref / Vapp on the approach;
- a flap bug at each detent's minimum manoeuvring speed (when to select the next notch);
- every bug scaled with √(current weight / the weight the profile's speeds were measured at);
- simplifications: V1 = VR minus a few kt (no runway-length calculation), V2 about 1.2 × the
  takeoff-flap stall speed;
- optional: a takeoff / landing data line on the new-flight screen;
- the chase-view readout (X3c) shows the next relevant bug as text.

## Data (found by the planner)

- **Reference weight.** The fleet harness measured every profile speed at the weight the stock
  JSBSim model ships with (`tests/fleet/README.md`, lines 41–42 and 103–104): c172p 2,350 lb,
  c310 about 4,400, global5000 about 80,000, 737 about 107,000, B747 about 551,000, A320 141,000,
  787-8 371,000, MD11 398,000, c182 about 2,100, pa28 about 1,950. Record the exact value per
  profile as `[speeds_kt] ref_weight_lbs` (step 1).
- **Current weight.** Not in `AircraftState` yet. JSBSim has it as `inertia/weight-lbs`.
- **Stall speed per detent.** Missing. F5 runs the idle-deceleration stall at every detent of the
  five airliners and records it as `[speeds_kt] vs_by_flap = …` (one per detent, at the reference
  weight; added to F5's card with this plan). For the other profiles (c172p, c182, pa28, c310,
  global5000) use the fallback in step 3.

## Do

1. **Weights.** Bind `inertia/weight-lbs` in `FlightModel` and copy it to
   `AircraftState::grossWeightLbs` each step. Add `[speeds_kt] ref_weight_lbs` to the loader and
   `AircraftProfile::refWeightLbs` (0 = no scaling). In the fleet stall phase print
   `FLEET <id> weight_lbs <value>` at the trimmed start with the default (AS LOADED) fuel, and write
   that value, rounded to 10 lb, into every profile with a comment naming the source.
2. **Speeds** (`SpeedBugs`, pure, no allocation): `k = sqrt(grossWeightLbs / refWeightLbs)`,
   1 when either is 0, clamped to 0.8 … 1.25.
   - `VR = vr × k`. Jets and turboprops: `V1 = VR − 5 kt`, `V2 = max(1.2 × Vs(takeoff detent) × k,
     VR + 3 kt)`. Pistons show VR only on takeoff (no V1 / V2).
   - `Vref = vref × k`, `Vapp = vapp × k`.
   - Flap bug for detent d: `1.3 × Vs(d) × k` (the minimum manoeuvring speed), labelled with the
     detent's label, never above `Vfe(d + 1)` (you select the next notch there).
3. **Stall speed per detent** `Vs(d)`: `vs_by_flap[d]` when the profile has it (F5); otherwise
   interpolate between `vs1` at the first detent and `vs0` at the last by the detent's
   `cmdNorm`.
4. **Which bugs show** (a small latched phase, unit-tested from state sequences):
   - **Takeoff**: on the ground with the throttle at 50 % or more, and after liftoff until the
     flaps are up or 3,000 ft AGL. V1 and VR until liftoff, V2 until the flaps are up; the flap bug
     of the next lower detent (when to retract).
   - **Approach**: airborne, not in Takeoff, and the flaps out or an approach loaded
     (`NavState`), or below 1.6 × Vref. Vref, Vapp, the current detent's flap bug and the next
     one down.
   - **Descent** (owner, 2026-11-09: "especially in the descent"): airborne, not Takeoff or
     Approach, VS below −300 fpm: the current detent's flap bug (UP = the minimum clean speed)
     and the next one down, so the pilot sees where to select the next notch.
   - **Otherwise** (cruise, taxi, after landing): only the current detent's flap bug while the
     flaps are out.
   - MIN declutter hides all of them (HUD.md §1); the AP speed bug draws on top of them.
5. **Drawing** in `drawSpeedTape`, inside the tape clip: a 10 px line out from the inner edge
   (`x1 … x1 + 10`) at the bug's speed, the label in TextSize::S at `x1 + 17` (right of the trend
   arrow), base HUD colour. Labels `V1`, `VR`, `V2`, `REF`, `APP`, flap bugs by detent label
   (`UP`, `5`, `15`). Bugs off the visible tape are not drawn.
6. **Chase view:** a `ChaseReadout::nextBug` line under the speed box at (16, 272), M size:
   the next bug ahead in the direction the speed is moving (`VR 145`, `V2 162`, `5 178`,
   `REF 140`), empty when none. Extend `hudChaseTest.cpp` so it stays clear of everything.
7. **Optional:** on the new-flight screen, a dim line under the list:
   `T/O  V1 140  VR 145  V2 162    LDG  VREF 140  VAPP 145` at the reference weight (exact for
   FUEL AS LOADED; label it `AT AS LOADED WEIGHT` for the other fuel choices, since the weight is
   only known once the flight starts).
