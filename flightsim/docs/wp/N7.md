# N7: Go-around (TOGA) and FLC

Phase 2 · Effort: high · Depends: none · Branch: `wp/N7-goaround-flc`

## Why

The game is about flying approaches down to minimums, but there is no go-around mode: a missed
approach is hand-flown until the pilot rebuilds the modes. FLC is already in the contract
(`VertMode::Flc` in `src/core/AutopilotState.h`) but not implemented (NAVIGATION §5.1 "Not
implemented in v1"), so airliner climbs and descents use VS.

## Do

1. Bindings: `GoAround` and `FlcMode` on free inputs of the L1 layer (Start and L3 are free there
   today; check `[pad.l1]` first). Never R3. Add keyboard keys too. The Help page picks them up
   from the live config.
2. Go-around, with the AP on or off (FD only):
   - A/T, if available: TOGA thrust. Otherwise the HUD message `SET TOGA THRUST`.
   - Pitch: `ga_pitch_deg` from the profile (default 15 for jets, 10 for light aircraft), with the
     existing AoA and underspeed protection.
   - Lateral: hold the current ground track (`GA TRK`).
   - Navigator: start the missed approach legs now if the active approach has them.
   - Above 400 ft AGL with a positive rate: lateral goes to NAV when missed legs exist, otherwise
     HDG. Vertical captures the missed-approach altitude, otherwise the altitude bug.
   - FMA shows `TOGA | GA TRK | GA`, then the normal modes.
3. FLC: the elevator holds the speed bug (IAS) toward the altitude bug. A/T sets
   `flc_climb_thrust_norm` (default 0.9) when climbing and idle when descending. ALT* captures as
   it does today. Without A/T, the HUD prompts the thrust setting.
4. Disconnect rules stay as they are (NAVIGATION §5).
