# N10b: Arcs at every fly-by turn

Phase 1.4 · Effort: high · Depends: N10, N11b · Branch: `wp/N10b-arcs-everywhere`

## Why

The owner (device session, 2026-11-10): the arcs from N10 still look like sharp corners on the
map and the route overlay. A diagnostic on the real KPDX–KOTH plan (c172p at 111 kt, fly-by radius
0.67 NM) found two causes:

1. **Turns with no arc at all.** `flyByEligible` (`src/nav/FlyBy.cpp`) gives an arc only when the
   outbound leg is a TF, and the inbound leg is a TF, IF or DF. So:
   - the turn at the last enroute fix onto the approach (the approach's first leg is an **IF** in
     the middle of the plan) is a sharp corner;
   - CIFP approach and procedure legs coded **CF** (course to a fix) or **DF** get none either,
     even when the CF's course is the straight line from the previous fix.
2. **Small turns, small arcs.** Enroute turns of 22–51° at 0.67 NM give only 0.1–0.3 NM of
   anticipation (`R tan(|turn| / 2)`), a slight bend at map ranges. That is the physical turn the AP
   flies, and what a Garmin draws.

This card makes every fly-by turn that the AP can fly as an arc get one: an IF in mid-plan, DF, and
a CF whose course matches the line from its previous fix. It keeps the physical radius (see
"Arc size" below).

## Do

1. **Eligibility** (`flyByEligible`, NAVIGATION 2.3). The fix at the end of `legs[fixLeg]` is flown
   by with an arc when it is an interior fix, not fly-over, both legs are on the same side of the
   missed approach, and both legs are "straight from the previous fix":
   - TF, and IF when it is not leg 0 (an IF in mid-plan is flown as a TF from the previous fix);
   - DF (the inbound side as today; as the outbound leg its line starts at T2, on the line from
     the fix to its own fix);
   - CF when `|wrap180(courseTrueDeg - bearing(previous fix -> its fix))| <= 5°`
     (`kCfStraightMaxDeg`). A CF that is not straight keeps today's behaviour: sequence at the fix,
     then intercept.
   Turns over 135° stay flown-over (unchanged).
2. **Sequencing** (`Navigator::shouldSequence`): ask `sequenceTurn` before the `flyOver` rule, so
   an eligible CF next leg starts its arc at T1 instead of being flown over. Course legs that are
   not eligible (CA, VA, FA, a non-straight CF) and holds keep the fly-over rule.
3. **Drawing:** nothing new. `legSpan` / `planTurnAt` already use `flyByEligible`, so the map, the
   mini nav and the route overlay draw the new arcs.
4. **Real-data diagnostic** (`tests/nav/flyByRealDataTest.cpp`, skipped unless
   `FSIM_REAL_DATA_DIR` is set). Load the nav pack, build the KPDX–KOTH plan from
   `assets/plans/KPDX-KOTH.fms` with its I05 approach (the CIFP provider, as the simulation does),
   and print one line per interior fix: ident, leg types in and out, turn (deg), eligible,
   radius and anticipation (NM) at 111 kt with the c172p bank. Check:
   - the fix before the approach's first leg (enroute → IF) has an arc;
   - every turn of 1–135° whose legs are TF, IF, DF or a straight CF has an arc.
5. **Docs:** NAVIGATION.md §2.3 lists the eligible leg types and the 5° CF rule.

### Arc size

Unchanged: the physical radius `R = V² / (g tan(bank))` at the fly-by bank, which is the turn the AP
flies and what the FD commands. A larger minimum radius would show small enroute turns more
clearly, but the AP would then fly a wider, earlier turn than the bank allows for. If the owner
wants that, it is one constant in `flyByRadiusNm` (a minimum radius) plus a test.
