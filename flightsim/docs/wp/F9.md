# F9: Pitch handling: stick shaping, trim step and rate, optional aids

Phase 1.4 · Effort: high · Depends: F5, F8 (share `assets/aircraft/*.ini`) ·
Branch: `wp/F9-pitch-handling`

## Why

The owner (2026-11-09): pitch, climb and descent are too sensitive to moderate, even with trim.
Holding ±100 fpm and changing the VS in 200 fpm steps should be easy in the C172, the PA28 and
the 737. Today the left stick goes through a radial deadzone 0.08 and `left_expo = 0.35`
(`assets/controls_default.ini` `[axes]`), then the user's PITCH SENSITIVITY / PITCH EXPO (both
identity by default), straight to `elevatorNorm` (full stick = full elevator at any speed). Trim
moves at one rate for every aircraft (`PilotTuning::trimRatePerS = 0.1`, 20 s end to end) while a
D-pad button is held; there is no step, so a tap moves it by whatever the frame time allows. The
AUTO TRIM setting exists (`Settings::assistAutoTrim`, a settings row) but nothing reads it.

## Do

1. **Measure before changing anything.** `FleetHandlingTest.cpp`, tests named
   `sim: fleet <id> handling`, for c172p, pa28 and 737 (print-only for the other seven). From a
   trimmed `cruise` start and a trimmed `final` start (approach flaps), feed the elevator through
   the same shaping the game uses (a helper that runs a raw stick value through
   `applyRadialStick` with the `[axes]` deadzone and expo and the default `StickTuning`):
   - raw pitch steps of 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 and 0.5 held 3 s, then released: pitch rate at 1 s (°/s)
     and the VS change at 3 s and at 6 s (fpm);
   - one trim press (one frame down, as a tap), and trim held 1 s: the VS change after 10 s,
     stick free, throttle fixed;
   - the trim travel time end to end.
   Print each as `FLEET <id> handling_<what> <value>` and keep the before numbers for the PR.
2. **Targets** (proposed by the planner; tighten or move them with the measured data and say why
   in the PR), in the C172, the PA28 and the 737, cruise and approach:
   - raw 0.2 held 3 s: VS change 150–500 fpm, pitch rate under 2.5 °/s;
   - raw 0.1: under 250 fpm (so ±100 fpm corrections are a small nudge);
   - one trim tap: 30–150 fpm settled; trim held 1 s: 150–600 fpm;
   - full stick still reaches full elevator (rotation, flare and stall recovery keep their
     authority: the fleet takeoff, landing and stall tests stay green unchanged).
3. **Tune the shaping**, smallest change first:
   - a pitch-only expo: new `[axes] left_pitch_expo` (default = `left_expo`), applied to the
     left stick's Y after the radial deadzone, so roll keeps today's feel;
   - per profile `[controls] pitch_expo` (overrides `left_pitch_expo` for that aircraft, e.g.
     more for the 737 whose elevator is strong at cruise speed);
   - the user's PITCH EXPO and PITCH SENSITIVITY stay on top, unchanged.
4. **Trim step and rate.** A press moves the trim by one step at once (`trim_step_norm`); held
   past 0.3 s it moves at `trim_rate_norm_per_s`. Defaults in `PilotTuning`; per profile
   `[controls] trim_step_norm` and `trim_rate_norm_per_s`. Pick each aircraft's values from the
   measurement (one tap ≈ 50–100 fpm). The rudder trim keeps today's behaviour.
5. **Optional aids, not realistic, default off** (settings rows under AUTO TRIM, `settings.ini`
   keys, `docs/CONTROLS.md` lines):
   - `pitch_damper = false`: adds `−k · q` elevator (pitch-rate damping) while the stick is
     inside ±0.3, `k` per category; measure the phugoid before and after;
   - `assist_auto_trim` (the existing, unwired setting): with the stick centred for 1 s, the
     trim moves slowly to hold the flight-path angle the pilot left, like a fly-by-wire pitch
     hold; off while the AP is engaged (the AP already trims) and on the ground.
   Both live in `PilotControls` (pure, no allocation), unit-tested from state sequences.
6. Before / after traces: a CSV excerpt or a plot per aircraft (c172p, pa28, 737) of the raw 0.2
   step (t, raw stick, elevator, q, VS) in the PR.
