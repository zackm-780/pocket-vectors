GPWS voice clips (assets/audio/voice/*.wav)
===========================================
The spoken GPWS callouts were generated offline with CMU Flite 2.2 and its "rms" voice by
tools/audio/make_voice_clips.py (trimmed, band-limited, normalised and resampled to 22,050 Hz).
